# Wayfind Cloud
## Product Direction — AI Teammates for Small Businesses

**Status:** New product direction  
**Positioning:** Always-on AI teammates for small businesses  
**Pricing direction:** Starting from $5/month  
**Primary goal:** Help small businesses and solopreneurs get everyday business work done with AI teammates that operate in the cloud.

---

## 1. What We're Building

Wayfind Cloud is an always-on AI workforce for small businesses and solopreneurs.

Users create named AI Bots, give each one a job, connect their business tools, and let them handle recurring tasks and everyday operations.

Unlike a traditional chatbot that primarily responds to questions, Wayfind Bots are designed to complete work. They can access connected services, use a cloud computer, remember business context, execute multi-step workflows, and continue working in the background.

The goal is simple:

**Give a small business the capabilities of a bigger team without needing to hire more people.**

A business owner should be able to create an AI Finance Bot to reconcile expenses, an Operations Bot to contact vendors, or a Marketing Bot to prepare company announcements.

Each Bot has a role, memory, tools and reusable skills.

## 2. Why We're Changing Direction

Our original positioning focused heavily on providing an affordable alternative to consumer AI agents and subscriptions.

However, competing primarily on price is not a strong enough differentiation.

There is a more meaningful opportunity in small businesses.

Solopreneurs and small teams already use AI to help with real business tasks, but they often need to combine several tools, repeat instructions, upload documents manually and supervise every step.

Examples of work business owners already use AI for:

- Reconciling bank transactions with expenses, invoices and receipts.
- Preparing employee payslips and business documents.
- Contacting vendors and following up on outstanding invoices.
- Preparing expense reports and accounting documents.
- Generating infographics and social media announcements.
- Organizing business information and preparing reports.
- Monitoring crypto wallets and preparing stablecoin payments.

These are practical, recurring tasks with measurable time savings.

Instead of selling another AI assistant, Wayfind should help users build an AI team that takes responsibility for completing these workflows.

## 6. Real Business Workflows

We should prioritize complete workflows rather than individual AI features.

A workflow should have:

1. An input — documents, messages, business records or a user instruction.
2. Context — information retrieved from memory and connected services.
3. Actions — operations performed through tools and the cloud computer.
4. An output — a completed document, report, updated record or prepared action.
5. Approval — human confirmation when an action is sensitive or irreversible.
6. History — a record of what happened and what remains to be done.

### Workflow A: Monthly Expense Reconciliation

**User request:**

"Reconcile this month's expenses. Match the bank transactions with invoices, payment slips and receipts. Tell me what's missing."

**Expected result:**
- Retrieve bank transactions and accounting records.
- Read uploaded receipts and invoices.
- Match records using dates, amounts, vendors and references.
- Identify unmatched transactions and missing documents.
- Categorize expenses.
- Prepare a reconciliation report.
- Ask for review before changing accounting records.

### Workflow B: Company Announcement

**User request:**

"Prepare an announcement for our new product launch."

**Expected result:**
- Understand the product information and company context.
- Draft a press release.
- Generate a branded infographic.
- Prepare LinkedIn and X posts.
- Create translated versions.
- Present the materials for review.
- Publish only after approval and authorization.

### Workflow C: Vendor Follow-up

**User request:**

"Check which vendor invoices are still outstanding and follow up with them."

**Expected result:**
- Retrieve vendor bills and payment records.
- Identify outstanding invoices.
- Draft personalized vendor emails.
- Request missing information where necessary.
- Ask for approval before sending.
- Record responses and follow-up status.

### Workflow D: Stablecoin Payroll

**User request:**

"Prepare this month's USDC salary payments."

**Expected result:**
- Retrieve approved payroll information.
- Calculate payment amounts.
- Prepare a recipient and payment summary.
- Verify wallet addresses and network information.
- Request explicit authorization.
- Execute approved transfers through an authorized wallet integration.
- Record transaction hashes and prepare a payment report.

Financial transfers must never be executed solely on the basis of an unreviewed AI-generated calculation.

## 7. Integrations and Connectors

Wayfind should operate on top of existing business services rather than attempting to rebuild them.

Our first major integration target is **Zoho MCP**.

Official integration:
https://www.zoho.com/mcp/

Zoho provides MCP access across multiple business services. We should leverage its existing MCP capabilities instead of implementing and maintaining individual API integrations for every Zoho product.

### Zoho integration strategy

Build a thin Wayfind connector wrapper around Zoho MCP.

Responsibilities:
- Handle customer authorization and connection setup.
- Manage credentials securely.
- Discover available MCP tools.
- Normalize tool discovery and invocation.
- Expose connected Zoho capabilities to Wayfind Bots.
- Apply Wayfind-level permissions and approval rules.
- Maintain execution history and error handling.

Potential Zoho services include:
- Zoho Books — invoices, bills, expenses and accounting.
- Zoho Expense — expense reports and reimbursements.
- Zoho CRM — contacts, leads and sales activities.
- Zoho Mail — business correspondence.
- Zoho WorkDrive — business documents and files.
- Zoho Calendar — schedules and events.
- Zoho People / Payroll — employee and payroll-related operations, subject to regional availability and permissions.
- Zoho Inventory — products and inventory records.

The actual available tools depend on Zoho's MCP service configuration and the customer's authorization.

**Important:** Zoho is an integration, not our product identity. Users should see Wayfind as the AI team operating their business tools, not as a Zoho-specific AI assistant.

### Future integrations

Potential integrations should include:

- Google Workspace.
- Microsoft 365.
- Email and calendar services.
- Cloud storage and document services.
- Accounting and payment platforms.
- Social media platforms.
- Blockchain wallets and onchain data providers.
- MCP-compatible business services.

We should prefer existing APIs and MCP integrations whenever practical, using thin wrappers to provide a consistent interface to Bots.

## 8. Core Product Architecture

Wayfind's existing core capabilities remain important.

### AI Bots

Each Bot has:
- Name and avatar.
- Assigned role and instructions.
- Persistent memory.
- Available tools and integrations.
- Reusable skills.
- Execution history.

### Persistent Memory

Bots should remember useful business context across tasks.

Examples:
- Company information and business preferences.
- Vendor details and communication preferences.
- Document formats and templates.
- Recurring workflow instructions.
- Previous task outcomes and important decisions.

Memory should be useful, structured and maintainable. We should not simply accumulate unlimited conversation history.

### Cloud Computer

Each account has an isolated cloud computer shared by its Bots.

Bots can use the shared environment to:
- Access websites and browser sessions.
- Work with files and documents.
- Use connected web applications.
- Generate and process business materials.
- Continue supported tasks when the user is away.

The shared environment also allows Bots to reuse files and hand off work without requiring separate computers for every Bot.

### Skills

Skills turn successful tasks into reusable workflows.

For example:
- Monthly expense reconciliation.
- Vendor invoice follow-up.
- Japanese payslip generation.
- Product announcement creation.
- Weekly treasury reporting.

Users should be able to reuse and improve these workflows rather than explaining the same task every time.

### Routines

Routines allow Bots to perform recurring or event-triggered work.

Examples:
- Reconcile expenses at the end of each month.
- Prepare a weekly business report.
- Check outstanding vendor invoices every Monday.
- Generate a weekly social media content plan.
- Monitor treasury activity.

Always-on should mean that Bots can make progress independently when their configured routines or tasks are triggered, not that an expensive computer must run continuously.

### Approvals and Security

Bots should ask for confirmation before:
- Sending external communications when approval is required.
- Publishing social media content.
- Modifying important accounting records.
- Making payments or transferring assets.
- Performing irreversible actions.

Credentials, OAuth tokens and private keys must be protected through secure integrations and vault mechanisms, not exposed in ordinary Bot conversations.

## 9. Pricing Direction

We want Wayfind to remain accessible to individuals and small businesses.

**Target entry price: $5/month.**

We are considering an entry price of $5, $7 or $8, but we do not want to move the starting price to $10.

Pricing principles:
- Keep the entry plan affordable.
- Allow users to bring their own AI model credentials where supported.
- Include access to selected affordable models.
- Offer premium model access in a higher plan.
- Define clear included computer and model usage.
- Avoid unpredictable usage-based billing and automatic overages.
- Make premium model and infrastructure costs sustainable.

We should validate actual model and computer costs before finalizing usage allowances and plan limits.

The product should sell the value of an AI workforce, not just access to an AI model.

## 11. What We Should Not Build First

To keep the product focused, avoid spending early development time on:

- A full accounting or ERP system.
- A replacement for Zoho or other business platforms.
- A general-purpose voice agent or voice-first stand-up system.
- Autonomous hiring decisions.
- Fully autonomous payroll and payment execution.
- A large collection of disconnected demo Bots.
- A marketplace before we have useful, repeatable workflows.
- Enterprise-only features that do not serve our initial target market.

We should focus on workflows that our existing cloud computer, memory, skills and MCP architecture can reliably support.

## 12. Product Principles

Every feature should follow these principles:

**1. Work, not conversation.**  
Bots should produce useful business outputs, not just provide advice.

**2. Small-business first.**  
Prioritize tasks that founders and small teams perform repeatedly but cannot justify hiring dedicated staff to handle.

**3. Use existing business tools.**  
Integrate with the services businesses already use rather than rebuilding their systems.

**4. Persistent context.**  
Bots should remember business preferences and previous work.

**5. Reusable workflows.**  
A successful task should be repeatable as a skill or routine.

**6. Background execution.**  
Bots should be able to continue supported work when the user is not actively chatting.

**7. Human control.**  
Sensitive operations and irreversible actions must have appropriate authorization and approval.

**8. Affordable by design.**  
Keep the entry price accessible while controlling infrastructure and model costs.
